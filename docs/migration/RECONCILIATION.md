## 6. Explained differences

- channel memberships exceed the raw legacy channel_members rows by design: legacy showed every public channel to every workspace member (computed), the new model stores that as rows.
- legacy threads without any reply (34 threads in total) are ordinary messages in the new model; "thread roots" counts roots that have replies.
- reactions on deleted legacy messages are dropped with the message body (the new tombstone keeps none).
- auth sessions and refresh tokens change in legacy whenever someone refreshes; their counts are compared against the snapshot this verify run read.
- the two merged DMs are left out of the DM unread check: one cursor cannot hold two legacy cursors, so the merged history counts as read up to the first message unread in either conversation (never hides an unread message).
