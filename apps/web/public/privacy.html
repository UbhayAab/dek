Dek privacy notice

Last updated 3 October 2026. Dek is the internal team chat of Jarurat Care Foundation and Safalta Setu.

This notice says, in plain words, what Dek collects, why, where it is kept and what you can do about it. It is written for India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the DPDP Rules, 2025.

Who is responsible

Your organization (Jarurat Care Foundation or Safalta Setu) decides why your data is processed and manages your membership. Questions about your account go to your organization's administrator first.

What Dek collects and why

WhatWhy
Your name, email address, and the profile details you choose to add (handle, title, time zone, photo, status)So colleagues know who they are talking to, and so you can sign in
Messages, threads, reactions, files, images, voice notes and videos you shareTeam communication; your organization keeps them as work records
Read positions, notification settings, the devices you turn notifications on for, and when you were last activeShowing what is unread on every device, delivering the notifications you asked for, showing whether a colleague is around
Calls: who joined and whenShowing call state and history lines in a conversation. Calls are never recorded; audio and video travel directly between devices or through an encrypted relay.
Reports and moderation actionsKeeping the workspace safe; administrators review reports

Dek has no advertising, no analytics trackers and does not sell or share data for marketing.

Where it is kept, and who processes it

  • Database, files and sign-in: Supabase, in the Mumbai region (AWS ap-south-1).
  • The app itself and the call relay: Cloudflare.
  • Sign-in code emails: Jarurat Care's own mail service on Amazon SES.
  • Notifications: your browser's or phone's push service (Google, Apple, Microsoft or Mozilla) carries the notification text. You can choose in Settings to show only the sender, or nothing, on your lock screen.

Only members of your organization who can see a conversation can read it. Files are private and opened through links that expire after 15 minutes.

How long it is kept

Messages and files stay until they are deleted by their author, removed by a moderator, or deleted under a retention rule your organization sets. There is no automatic deletion schedule today. Notification delivery records are kept for 14 days. Backups are kept for 7 days.

Your rights

You can see and correct your profile in Settings at any time. You can ask your administrator for a copy of your data, for corrections, or for erasure, and you can nominate someone to act for you. You can delete your own messages. You can delete your whole account yourself: see deleting your Dek account. Grievances are answered within 90 days, as the DPDP Rules allow; contact your administrator first.

Children

Dek is a workplace tool for people invited by their organization. It is not directed at children.

Security and breaches

Encrypted connections everywhere, access checked on every request on the server, private files with short-lived links, and notifications that respect your preview setting. If a breach affects you, you and the Data Protection Board will be told without delay, with a full report within 72 hours.
