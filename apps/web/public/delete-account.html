Delete your Dek account

Dek, the team chat of Jarurat Care Foundation and Safalta Setu.

Delete it yourself (takes a minute)

  1. Open Dek and sign in (dek-7o4.pages.dev, or the Dek app on your phone).
  2. Go to Settings, then Account.
  3. Choose Delete account, type DELETE to confirm, and press the button.

Your account is deleted at once and you are signed out on every device.

If you cannot sign in

Ask an administrator of your organization to remove you (Admin, Members, Remove). That ends your access immediately. They can also ask for your account to be deleted on your behalf.

What is deleted, and what your organization keeps

  • Deleted: your sign-in, your name, handle, photo, title and status, your memberships, your notification devices and settings, and your saved messages.
  • Kept by your organization as work records: messages and files you posted in shared conversations. They stay where they were, shown as written by "Deleted user". You can delete any of your own messages before deleting your account.
  • Kept for safety: moderation records (reports and administrator actions), which only organization administrators can see.
  • Backups that may contain your data roll off within 7 days.

See the privacy notice for everything else.
